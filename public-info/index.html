PERSONAL INTEGRATION

Oura Notifications

A private, single-user app that turns synced Oura measurements into concise notifications delivered through ntfy.

How it works

The account owner authorizes access through Oura. The app reads available sleep, readiness, activity, stress, SpO2, and workout information, creates factual summaries, and sends updates to the owner's configured ntfy topic.

This is a personal integration, not a public service. It has no public registration and is not affiliated with or endorsed by Oura. Measurements and summaries are not medical advice.
